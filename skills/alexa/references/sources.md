## Usage Notes

- Prefer Amazon Help and Alexa Skills Kit docs over third-party blogs for product steps and certification blockers.
- Treat manufacturer skill-linking steps as brand-specific; confirm in the Alexa app and the device maker's current docs.
- Privacy and voice-history controls change by region and account settings; verify the live Alexa app Privacy screens when advising deletion or auto-delete windows.
