# Domain Knowledge — New York (State)

Use this file for sourced statewide facts that change advice across regions. Prefer the official URL when a detail is mutable.

## Congestion pricing and downstate driving cost

- New York's Congestion Relief Zone / CBD Tolling Program began charging most passenger vehicles entering Manhattan's Central Business District below 60th Street on 2025-01-05.
- Treat toll amount, exemptions, credits, and payment methods as mutable; verify current rules before quoting a number.
- Official program page: https://www.mta.info/tolls/congestion-relief-zone
- Official overview and related notices: https://www.mta.info/project/CBDTP

## REAL ID timing

- TSA currently describes May 7, 2025 as the date when travelers need a REAL ID-compliant credential or another accepted alternative for domestic commercial flights.
- For New York movers and travelers, verify document lists and Enhanced Driver License options on DMV pages rather than assuming every license already qualifies.
- TSA REAL ID page: https://www.tsa.gov/real-id
- New York DMV REAL ID overview: https://dmv.ny.gov/driver-license/get-real-id-driver-license-or-non-driver-id-card

## Climate and energy policy pressure

- New York's Climate Leadership and Community Protection Act (CLCPA) sets statewide greenhouse-gas and renewable-electricity targets that affect building electrification, utility planning, and long-term energy costs.
- Treat local utility rates and building codes as region-specific overlays on the statewide statute.
- Climate Act overview: https://climate.ny.gov/
- CLCPA statute text: https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/laws/ENV/A75

## Housing and eviction frameworks

- Statewide rent regulation and Good Cause Eviction frameworks vary by municipality and building type. Do not generalize NYC rules to every New York locality.
- Confirm coverage, notice requirements, and local opt-in status before advising landlords or tenants.
- Homes and Community Renewal rent regulation: https://hcr.ny.gov/rent-regulation
- Good Cause Eviction overview: https://hcr.ny.gov/good-cause-eviction
