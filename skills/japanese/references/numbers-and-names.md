# Numbers, Dates, Names, and Addresses

Japanese large numbers group by four: `1,200,000 → 120万`, `3,500,000,000 → 35億`. Reiwa year is western year minus 2018 (`2026 → 令和8年`). Select counters by the noun: `本` long/thin, `枚` flat, `台` machines, `冊` bound volumes, `匹` small animals, `頭` large animals; read irregular forms carefully (`いっぽん`, `さんぼん`, `ろっぽん`).

Do not guess a person's name reading. Copy a confirmed reading or ask once. Format Japanese addresses large-to-small, and preserve the requested document's date and numeral convention.
