# Japanese Typography

Use full-width Japanese punctuation: `、。 「」 『』 （） ？！`. Japanese ellipsis is `……`; a long dash is `——`. Do not add a space after Japanese punctuation. Use `「」` for speech and terms, `『』` for titles or nested quotation; do not import Chinese `《》` for ordinary Japanese titles.

The chat exception is deliberate: a final `。` can feel distant in casual LINE-style messages. Use the recipient and channel, not a blanket rule. Half-width katakana is legacy encoding and should not appear in normal user-facing prose.
