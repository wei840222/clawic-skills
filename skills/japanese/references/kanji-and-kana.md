# Kanji, Kana, and Okurigana

Use the user or publication's house style first. In ordinary prose, choose readable kanji density rather than maximal kanji. Common function words are normally opened: `こと`, `もの`, `とき`, `ため`, `ところ`, `できる`, `ください`, `さらに`, `ただし`, `たくさん`. Keep auxiliary `いただく` in kana; use `頂く` only for physically receiving.

Use one okurigana convention consistently. Add furigana only for names or genuinely difficult words when the audience needs it. Do not convert every familiar word to kana or every word to rare kanji.
