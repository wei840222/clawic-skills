# Slang and Currency

Treat slang as audience- and date-dependent. `やばい`, `えぐい`, `神`, `りょ`, and `とりま` all signal age, platform, region, and closeness; use plain language when any of those are unknown. Do not put casual slang in external mail, formal documents, or messages upward in an organisation.

When a term's current meaning or currency is uncertain, verify it or avoid it. Preserve a user's established wording when it is clearly intentional; do not “correct” a community's dialect into a different persona.
