# Spoken Japanese

Spoken Japanese is shorter than written Japanese and allows repetition, fragments, and backchannels. For a call, open with identity and availability, state the purpose, then confirm the next action. For a speech, use short breath-length units and rehearse names and numbers aloud.

Ceremonial copy needs a separate check for 忌み言葉: avoid repetition words in funerals and separation words at weddings. Do not fabricate personal memories or congratulations; retain supplied facts and use placeholders for the rest.
