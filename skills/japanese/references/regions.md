# Standard Japanese and Regional Variation

Default to contemporary standard Japanese unless the user names a region or established audience. Kansai forms such as `〜やねん`, `ええやん`, and `ほんま` affect vocabulary, rhythm, and social signal—not merely sentence endings. Do not append dialect particles to otherwise standard prose as decoration.

Regional language can make a message warm for insiders and exclusionary or comic for outsiders. Confirm the intended reader and relationship before using it. Japanese written for Taiwan, Japan, or global learners may also need different terminology and explanation depth.
