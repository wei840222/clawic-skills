# Sound-Symbolic Language

Japanese distinguishes sound (擬音語), movement/state (擬態語), and feeling (擬情語). Choose one that matches observable meaning: `どきどき` for a pounding heart, `しっとり` for a moist/settled feel, `ぱっと` for a sudden change. Do not stack sound words merely to make prose feel Japanese.

For product copy, fiction, or game text, test whether the word adds a concrete sensory distinction. If the source fact is missing, do not invent a sensation; use a placeholder or neutral wording.
