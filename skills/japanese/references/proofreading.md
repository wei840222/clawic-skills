# Proofreading Japanese

Review in passes: (1) recipient and register, (2) names/readings and honorific direction, (3) grammar and dropped subjects, (4) typography, numbers, and encoding, (5) natural rhythm aloud. Search for half-width katakana, `...`, `--`, ASCII punctuation, repeated `私は`, repeated hedges, and `させていただく`.

Check orthographic consistency: the same term, kanji/kana choice, date format, and honorific should not drift. Preserve quoted source text and proper names unless the user asks to normalize them.
