# Berlin research sources and current guidance

Load this reference whenever advice depends on a time-sensitive legal, immigration, registration, or fare claim. Check the linked official page again before quoting a price, deadline, or eligibility threshold, because these values change.

## Residence registration (Anmeldung)

- **Berlin.de — Moving to Berlin: Registration Offices** — https://www.berlin.de/en/life/new-in-berlin/744279-8206946-moving-to-berlin-registration-offices.en.html — A person moving to Berlin or changing apartments must register or re-register within 14 days of moving in; the page identifies the Bürgeramt appointment process and required identity/landlord documentation.

## EU Blue Card (2026)

- **Bundesagentur für Arbeit — EU Blue Card** — https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/vor-ort/zav/working-and-living-in-germany/iss-en/issue-03-2026/blue-card — For 2026, the regular minimum gross annual salary is EUR 50,700. The reduced threshold is EUR 45,934.20 for eligible shortage occupations, new entrants, and qualifying IT specialists; the threshold is adjusted annually and the reduced route requires Federal Employment Agency approval.

## Berlin fares (checked 2026-09-07)

- **BVG — All tickets at a glance** — https://www.bvg.de/en/subscriptions-and-tickets/all-tickets — Current ticket catalogue lists fares and validity rules. A non-subscription monthly ticket starts at EUR 113. Consult the applicable ticket page and fare zone before quoting a specific fare.

## Cannabis Act

- **Federal Ministry of Health — Frequently asked questions on the Cannabis Act** — https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/en/themen/cannabis/faq-cannabis-act — Official starting point for current CanG permissions and youth-protection restrictions; check it rather than describing cannabis use as generally tolerated or unrestricted.
