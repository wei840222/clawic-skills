## Scenario results

2. **Non-EU software engineer:** The workflow separates Blue Card eligibility from annual thresholds and routes registration details to Berlin's official service guidance.
3. **Cannabis question:** The workflow uses the Federal Ministry of Health source and preserves conditional permissions and restrictions.

## Limits

The score is a dry-run structural assessment. It is not a claim of live legal or immigration advice accuracy, and live values must still be checked through the official links in `research-sources.md`.
