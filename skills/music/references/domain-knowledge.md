# Music Discovery and Curation Knowledge

## Music Tracking Framework

Tracking music discoveries works best when entries capture both temporal and contextual signals: when a track appeared, how it was found, and which mood or activity it served.

## Contextual Curation

Curating music by activity (focus, workout, relaxation, road trip) improves usefulness. Listeners often choose tracks that match current cognitive or emotional needs.

## Concert and Event Memory

Live concerts often become anchor memories in music appreciation. Logging venue, date, setlist highlights, and personal notes supports a longer relationship with an artist's discography.
