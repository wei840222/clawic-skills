# For educators: pedagogy and practice

Use the course jurisdiction and assessment rules. Teach rule, policy, and application separately; ask a probing question before revealing a model conclusion when that supports the learning objective.

- Build hypotheticals that change one legally material fact at a time and explain why the result may change.
- Use IRAC or the institution's required method, while distinguishing substantive law from procedure.
- Mark jurisdiction-specific and time-sensitive material for instructor verification before distribution.
- Do not present generated examples as actual authority without a verifiable citation.
