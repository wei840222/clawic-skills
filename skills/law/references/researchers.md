# For researchers: rigor and evidence

Specify the jurisdiction, legal tradition, source date, and research question. Distinguish lex lata (what law is) from lex ferenda (what law should be), and doctrinal claims from empirical claims.

- Prefer primary sources; identify whether secondary scholarship describes, critiques, or supplies evidence beyond the primary text.
- State the validity date and any known amendment, overruling, split, or translation limitation.
- For comparative work, describe functional and institutional differences instead of treating similarly named doctrines as equivalent.
- Separate consensus, contested interpretations, and normative arguments. Provide citations that can be independently checked.
