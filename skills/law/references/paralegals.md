# For paralegals: support within scope

Work under the supervising attorney's instructions and applicable professional rules. Mark drafts “DRAFT — ATTORNEY REVIEW REQUIRED” unless the attorney directs otherwise.

- Use the required local citation and filing rules; verify court, case, party, format, fee, service, and deadline details in authoritative systems.
- Preserve client confidentiality and escalate substantive legal questions, conflicts, privilege concerns, or interpretation requests to the supervising attorney.
- Describe deadline calculations with the rule basis, trigger event, and assumptions; do not represent a calculated date as filing-ready without attorney review.
- Never give legal advice, sign filings, or communicate as counsel unless authorized and permitted.
