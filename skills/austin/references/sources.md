# Sources — Austin

Use these sources when cost, law, transit, weather, or employer facts may have moved. Prefer primary portals over blog roundups for high-stakes answers.

## City and Regional Services

- City of Austin official site: https://www.austintexas.gov/
- Austin Public Health: https://www.austintexas.gov/department/austin-public-health
- Austin Water / drought and watering rules: start from https://www.austintexas.gov/department/austin-water
- Travis County services hub: https://www.traviscountytx.gov/

## Transit and Mobility

- CapMetro trip planning and alerts: https://www.capmetro.org/
- CapMetro MetroRail information: https://www.capmetro.org/metrorail
- Austin-Bergstrom International Airport (AUS): https://www.austintexas.gov/airport

## State Rules that Bind Austin Residents

- Texas.gov resident services: https://www.texas.gov/
- Texas DPS driver license services: https://www.dps.texas.gov/section/driver-license
- TxDMV new-to-Texas vehicle guidance: https://www.txdmv.gov/motorists/new-to-texas
- Texas Comptroller taxes portal: https://comptroller.texas.gov/
- Texas property tax help: https://comptroller.texas.gov/taxes/property-tax/
- Power to Choose (deregulated electricity areas): https://www.powertochoose.org/

## Housing and Cost Orientation

- U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics CPI / regional data: https://www.bls.gov/cpi/
- HUD fair market rents and housing resources: https://www.hud.gov/
- Local MLS / major listing portals for live rent and sale quotes (Zillow, Redfin, realtor.com, or brokerage feeds) — treat as market snapshots, not official stats
- Travis Central Appraisal District for assessed-value and property-tax context: https://www.traviscad.org/

## Weather, Allergies, and Hazards

- National Weather Service Austin/San Antonio: https://www.weather.gov/ewx/
- NOAA Climate Data Online: https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/cdo-web/
- FEMA flood maps when flood risk matters: https://msc.fema.gov/
- Texas Ready preparedness: https://www.texasready.gov/
- AAA Texas pollen / allergy context often referenced by locals; pair with clinician guidance rather than self-diagnosis

## Work, Immigration, and Business (informational)

- U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services: https://www.uscis.gov/
- Texas Secretary of State business filings: https://www.sos.state.tx.us/
- Texas Workforce Commission: https://www.twc.texas.gov/
- Employer career pages (Tesla, Oracle, Dell, Apple, Google, etc.) for current hiring footprint — never invent headcount

## Culture, Events, and Music

- Official Visit Austin tourism portal: https://www.austintexas.org/
- SXSW official site: https://www.sxsw.com/
- Austin City Limits Festival: https://www.aclfestival.com/
- Do512 event calendar: https://do512.com/
- Austin Chronicle listings: https://www.austinchronicle.com/

## Usage Notes

- Unstable numbers (rent, gas, salaries, wait times) need a fresh source check before financial or travel commitments.
- Legal and immigration items are orientation only; licensed professionals own filings and representation.
- When statewide Texas process detail dominates, load the `texas` skill and its sources rather than duplicating DPS/TxDMV depth here.
