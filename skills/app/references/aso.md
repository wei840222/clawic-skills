# App Store Optimization (ASO)

## Listing and discovery

- Put a clear, relevant primary term in the title; use the subtitle or short description for a distinct benefit and secondary terms.
- On iOS, avoid duplicating title terms in the keyword field; use commas without added spaces.
- Localize metadata and creative for each supported market, then measure conversion and ranking separately by locale.
- Use Apple Custom Product Pages or Google Play Custom Store Listings when a campaign needs audience-specific creative.

## Creative and reviews

- Lead screenshots and preview media with the user outcome, not an internal feature label. Make essential video information understandable without sound.
- Test one creative hypothesis at a time and retain a control so listing experiments remain interpretable.
- Request ratings after a user has completed a meaningful positive action. Respect platform quotas and do not offer incentives for ratings or reviews.
- Respond to substantive negative reviews with a support path; never expose account or diagnostic data publicly.
