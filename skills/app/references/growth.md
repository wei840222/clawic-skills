# Growth and acquisition

## Referral loops

- Give both sender and recipient a clear, policy-compliant benefit; track the complete path from invite to activated user.
- Use deep links to route installed users to the intended content and fall back safely for new users.
- Measure invite rate, acceptance rate, activation rate, and fraud/abuse rate before scaling incentives.

## Paid acquisition

- Establish an attribution window, a conversion event, and a budget guardrail before spending.
- Compare incremental lift with baseline demand; do not infer causality from installs alone.
- Refresh creative when frequency or performance data show fatigue, and build lookalikes from qualified or paying users where policy permits.
- Scale only when contribution margin and cohort retention support the projected CAC payback period.
