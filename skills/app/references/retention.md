# Retention and launch

## Retention

- Define the activation event that predicts durable value, then use cohort retention curves to identify the earliest material drop-off.
- Improve onboarding before expanding acquisition spend; the first session should lead quickly to the product's relevant "aha" moment.
- Use notifications only with consent, a user-relevant purpose, sensible frequency caps, and a measurable re-engagement hypothesis.
- Evaluate streaks, reminders, and reactivation campaigns for durable value rather than short-term opens alone.

## Launch readiness

- Validate the funnel, support coverage, instrumentation, and rollback plan in a limited release before a broad launch.
- Coordinate store assets, support, communications, and measurement definitions; record a baseline before launch activity begins.
- Prioritize the supported platforms and markets indicated by product evidence rather than generic market-share assumptions.
