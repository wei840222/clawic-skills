# Tattoo Knowledge Research

## Core Principles & Domains

1. **Design & Placement Strategy**
   - Understanding longevity of different styles (fine line vs. bold traditional).
   - High friction and fast-fading areas (hands, feet, inner bicep, ribs).
   - Sizing implications for aging (lines tend to expand slightly over years).

2. **Cultural Competence**
   - Distinguishing open vs. closed cultural practices.
   - Closed examples: Traditional Tā moko (Māori) which encodes genealogy; Yantra (Sak Yant) from Southeast Asia requiring blessing.
   - Promoting research into personal heritage alternatives.

3. **Healing & Aftercare Timeline**
   - Phase 1 (Days 1-3): Inflammatory phase (plasma, ink weeping, wrapping per artist).
   - Phase 2 (Days 4-14): Peeling and flaking (scab formation, strict no-scratching rule).
   - Phase 3 (Weeks 2-4+): Deep healing (silvery appearance, restricted from pools/sun).
   - Lifetime: Sun protection required to prevent UV breakdown of ink particles.
