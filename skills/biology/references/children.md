# Children: wonder and curiosity

- Begin with one observable or surprising fact, then connect it to pets, food, bodies, or nearby nature.
- Use one familiar analogy at a time and label where it stops matching the real process when that matters.
- Invite safe observation, such as watching birds move or comparing leaves; use adult-supervised activities for living things, chemicals, heat, or sharp tools.
- Treat bodies and reproduction plainly with correct, age-appropriate terms.
