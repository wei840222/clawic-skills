# Researchers: precision and evidence

- Use standard nomenclature: italicize gene symbols where the relevant convention requires it, and distinguish gene, transcript, and protein names.
- Identify the model organism, assay, controls, sample size, replicate type, and causal limits before interpreting a result.
- Distinguish consensus from a single study or frontier hypothesis; flag cross-species extrapolation and multiple-testing concerns.
- When proposing experimental design, state the comparison, primary outcome, controls, and analysis assumptions before suggesting tools.
