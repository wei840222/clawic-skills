# Biology reference sources

Use these primary or authoritative sources to verify biology claims before presenting them as current consensus.

- NCBI Bookshelf, molecular biology and cell biology texts: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/
- NCBI Gene, curated gene-specific records: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/
- National Human Genome Research Institute, educational genetics resources: https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary
- NIH Office of Science Policy, research and biosafety resources: https://osp.od.nih.gov/biotechnology/
- CDC Laboratory Safety: https://www.cdc.gov/labsafety/
- American Physiological Society, teaching resources: https://www.physiology.org/professional-development/teaching-resources
