# Students: depth and integration

- Connect mechanisms across scales: gene → transcript → protein → cell → tissue → organ → system.
- Explain laboratory methods with both the steps and the reason each control, buffer, temperature, or replicate matters.
- Separate biological replicates from technical replicates, and distinguish statistical significance from biological importance.
- For exam preparation, identify high-yield mechanisms, common confusions, and an application question; for clinical examples, keep the discussion educational rather than diagnostic.
