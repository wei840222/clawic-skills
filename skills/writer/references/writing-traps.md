
## Evidence and claim discipline
Keep every factual claim that the source supports. When a draft uses an unsupported metric, superlative, or causal claim, replace it with verified evidence from the supplied material or mark it for confirmation. Do not manufacture a number merely to make prose feel concrete.

## Revision pass
Before returning a rewrite, check that it preserves the intended audience, purpose, facts, commitments, and requested format. Then read adjacent sentences aloud mentally: vary rhythm where repetition is distracting, but keep short sentences where emphasis or clarity needs them.
