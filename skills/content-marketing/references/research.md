# Content marketing research

## Foundational definition and strategy

- **Content Marketing Institute, "What Is Content Marketing?"** — defines content marketing as creating and distributing valuable, relevant, and consistent content to attract and retain a clearly defined audience and drive profitable customer action. https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/what-is-content-marketing
- **Google Search Central, "Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content"** — use a people-first purpose, demonstrate first-hand expertise where relevant, and avoid content created mainly to manipulate rankings. https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content

## Measurement and experimentation

- **Google Analytics documentation, "[GA4] About key events"** — mark the meaningful user actions for a business as key events and use consistently defined events to measure outcomes. https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9355838
- **Google Analytics documentation, "[GA4] Attribution models"** — attribution assigns conversion credit using a selected model; interpret channel results with the configured model and reporting scope. https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/10597962

## Application notes

- Define the audience, desired action, distribution channel, and measurement event before publishing.
- Treat format mixes and repurposing volume as hypotheses rather than universal ratios; test them against audience and channel evidence.
- Recheck platform rules, ranking guidance, and analytics configuration when a task depends on a current product behavior.
