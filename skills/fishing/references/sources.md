# Sources — Fishing

Use these sources when tackle selection, species behavior, catch-and-release handling, or local regulation context matters. Prefer primary agency and extension guidance over marketing blogs.

## Tackle and technique

- Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission — Fishing gear and techniques overview: https://myfwc.com/fishing/saltwater/recreational/gear/
- Take Me Fishing — How to choose fishing line: https://www.takemefishing.org/how-to-fish/how-to-catch-fish/how-to-choose-fishing-line/
- Take Me Fishing — Hook types and sizes: https://www.takemefishing.org/how-to-fish/how-to-catch-fish/fishing-hooks/
- U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service — Freshwater fishing basics: https://www.fws.gov/story/freshwater-fishing-basics

## Species and habitat

- U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service — Fish and aquatic conservation: https://www.fws.gov/program/fish-and-aquatic-conservation
- NOAA Fisheries — Recreational fishing: https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/topic/recreational-fishing
- Florida Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission — Freshwater fish identification: https://myfwc.com/fishing/freshwater/fish/

## Catch handling and ethics

- Keep Fish Wet — Best practices for catch-and-release: https://www.keepfishwet.org/
- NOAA Fisheries — Catch and release best practices: https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/national/resources-fishing/catch-and-release-best-practices

## Regulations note

- License rules, seasons, bag limits, and gear restrictions are jurisdiction-specific. Always confirm the current state/provincial wildlife agency page for the water being fished before advising legal limits.

## Usage notes

- Prefer agency and extension sources for regulations, seasons, and species ID.
- Tackle brand claims are secondary; match recommendations to the angler's logged inventory in `<state_root>/memory.md`.
- Weather, water temperature, and pressure patterns are local; treat historical catches in `<state_root>/catches.md` as the primary personalization signal.
