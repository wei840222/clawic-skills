# Primary Sources

Use these sources when a real-estate investing question needs current, jurisdiction-specific, or lending-program facts. Verify terms that affect an actual decision against the applicable lender, regulator, insurer, or local authority.

- U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development, FHA Single Family Housing Policy Handbook: https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/OCHCO/documents/4000.1hsgh-032026.pdf
- Federal Housing Finance Agency, Enterprise Regulatory Capital Framework (DSCR terminology and mortgage-risk context): https://www.fhfa.gov/policy/regulatory-and-governance/regulatory-activities/enterprise-regulatory-capital-framework
- Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, mortgage resources: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/consumer-tools/mortgages/
- Federal Emergency Management Agency, Flood Map Service Center: https://msc.fema.gov/portal/home
